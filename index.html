Handla

The shared shopping list that keeps up with you.

What it does

  • Add items one-handed — the keyboard stays up so your whole list goes in fast.
  • Changes appear live on every phone sharing the list. No refresh, no sync button.
  • Smart suggestions learn what you buy, so most items are one tap away.
  • Group your list by aisle so you're not backtracking across the store.
  • Share in one tap with an invite link.
  • Sign in with Apple — no password, and delete your account any time.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Handla is a collaborative shopping list app. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Handla does not track you and does not share your data with third parties for advertising.

What we collect

  • Email address — provided through Sign in with Apple, used only to identify your account.
  • Display name — the name you choose, shown to people you share lists with.
  • List content — the items, quantities, and categories you add to your shopping lists.

How it's used

This data is used solely to make the app work: to sign you in, sync your lists across your devices in real time, and share lists with people you invite. It is not used for advertising or tracking, and it is not sold.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored on Supabase (our backend provider) and is protected by row-level security so that you can only access your own lists and the lists shared with you.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. This removes your profile, lists, and suggestions immediately.
